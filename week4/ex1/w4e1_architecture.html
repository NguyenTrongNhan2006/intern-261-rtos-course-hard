Mode-switch FSM (LO ↔ HI ↔ watchdog reset)

CPU_OVERLOAD fires at jitter > 100 µs (alarm, below the 113 µs hard limit) or total CPU > 76 %. In HI mode the heartbeat mask drops the shed roles so the watchdog is not tripped by design.
